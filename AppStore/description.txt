Frame Mat - offline photo mat-cut planning.

Plan single- or double-mat cuts with artwork, opening, border, board and frame dimensions. See a responsive preview, switch between millimeters and inches, and review calculated overlap and fit warnings before cutting.

Save named drafts on your device. Import or export a versioned JSON backup, or create a one-page PDF worksheet with cut dimensions, warnings and a physical-measurement reminder. The worksheet diagram is illustrative, not a 1:1 cutting template.

Designed for home framers and craft groups on iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets, and Windows. Your measurements stay local; there are no accounts or cloud sync. Frame Mat does not measure objects automatically or guarantee fit. Verify artwork, frame rebates, board and cutter tolerances with physical measurements before cutting.
